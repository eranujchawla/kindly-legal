Kindly — Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-09-26

Kindly helps you remember birthdays, anniversaries and other dates for the people you care about, and gets a message ready for you to send. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is kept and what control you have. Kindly is published by ACT ("we", "us"). We have written it to be true of the version of the app you are using. It takes effect on 26 September 2026.

The short version

Everything you add to Kindly is stored on your phone. This version of Kindly has no account and no server: we do not receive, see or store your information.

Kindly contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash-reporting services, and it does not use artificial intelligence (AI) to process your information.

A message leaves your phone only when you choose to send it: Kindly opens WhatsApp or your messaging app with the message filled in, and you press Send there. Kindly never sends a message by itself.

What Kindly stores, on your phone

About the people you add: their name, nickname, relationship to you and how they are linked to others in your family, phone number, email address, postal address, photo, notes you write, whether they are a favourite, the tones you allow for them, and dates such as birthdays and anniversaries, with the year if you know it.

The messages Kindly suggests and any you write or edit, the time you chose for each, which occasions you have opened a message for, and whether you told Kindly it was sent.

About you: the name and short description of your writing style you choose to enter, your own birthday if you add it, your usual tone, and your time zone (detected from your phone unless you choose one).

All of this is kept in the app’s private storage on your device. It is not copied to us or to anyone else.

Where the information comes from

From you, when you type it in.

From your contacts, only when you ask. "Choose from contacts" copies the details of the one contact you pick. "Import from contacts" shows your contacts so you can choose several, and copies only the ones you select: name, phone numbers, birthday and other dates, nickname, email, postal address and photo. Your contact list itself is never uploaded, and contacts you do not choose are not saved.

From your photo library, only for a photo you choose. The system photo picker gives Kindly only the picture you tap. Every photo is shrunk to a small square before it is saved.

Permissions

Contacts (Android: READ_CONTACTS). Asked for only after Kindly has explained why, when you choose or import contacts. You can refuse, and you can still add people by typing their details. You can withdraw the permission at any time in your phone’s settings.

Photos. Kindly uses the system photo picker, which needs no photo-library permission on current Android versions. On Android 12 and older, the picker uses the storage permission to read the photo you choose. Kindly does not use your camera or microphone.

Internet. Used only for the optional address search described below.

What leaves your phone

Messages you send. When you tap WhatsApp or Text, Kindly opens that app with the recipient and message filled in. What happens next — whether you send it, and how it is delivered — is between you and that app, under its own terms and privacy policy. If WhatsApp cannot be opened directly, Kindly opens WhatsApp’s web link (wa.me) in your browser instead, which passes the number and message to WhatsApp’s website so it can show them to you. Kindly is not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta.

Address search, where available. In versions of the app where address search is switched on, the address text you type is sent to Geoapify, our address-lookup provider, to suggest matching addresses, together with your country (taken from your phone’s language settings) to rank the results. Like any website, Geoapify also receives your device’s internet (IP) address. No name, phone number or other detail about the person goes with it. Where address search is not available, you type the address in and nothing is sent.

Nothing else. Kindly does not sell, rent or share your information, and does not send it to us.

Information about other people

Kindly stores details about people in your life, including children if you add them. Please add only information you have a reasonable right to keep and use to stay in touch with them.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your information stays on your phone until you delete it.

You can change or remove details at any time in the app — for example an address, a photo, a link between people, or every saved phone number at once (Settings → Your data → Remove all phone numbers).

Settings → Your data → Delete everything permanently removes everyone and everything you have added, including your own details and any photo copies the app cached. It cannot be undone, because there is no copy anywhere else.

Uninstalling Kindly, or clearing its storage in your phone’s settings, also deletes all of it.

Security

Your information is kept in the app’s private storage, which other apps cannot read, and it is excluded from your phone’s cloud backup. Because it is not transmitted to us, there is no copy on a server to be exposed. Anyone who can unlock your phone can open the app, so please protect your phone with a lock.

Children

Kindly is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not designed for or directed at children.

Your rights

Under Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) you have the right to know what personal information is held about you, to have it corrected and to withdraw your consent. Because Kindly keeps your information only on your phone, you can see, correct and delete all of it yourself in the app.

ACT is responsible for this policy and for how Kindly handles personal information. If you have a question, a request or a concern about privacy, write to our privacy contact at the address below; we will reply within 30 days. You may also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca).

Features that are not switched on

We may later offer optional backup of your information, or messages written with the help of AI. Neither is part of this version. Before either is switched on we will update this policy, tell you in the app what would change, and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, for any significant change, tell you in the app. The current version is always available in the app under Settings → Privacy policy and at eranujchawla.github.io/kindly-legal.
